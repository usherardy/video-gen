When I was in eighth grade, I decided I wanted to build a company.
I didn't know how, or what it would become. I just knew I wanted to create something real.
Then life took me to Lithuania.
A new country, a new chapter, and a different kind of struggle.
But living abroad was hard.
The pressure of money, survival and not knowing what comes next never stopped.
Some nights it felt less like a dream and more like a nightmare.
But somewhere inside that pressure, I realized something.
Building was never just something I liked doing. It felt like freedom.
Every app, every project, every late night at my desk was a small attempt to build my way out.
Out of limits. Out of fear. Out of a life run by survival.
So I kept building.
Games first, then websites, robots, ideas. Some worked. Most didn't.
But I couldn't stop.
Maybe building is more than ambition for me.
Maybe it's the only thing that ever made the future feel possible.
I still don't know which idea will change everything.
But I know this. I owe it to the boy I was to keep going.
Follow along. The story is still being written.
